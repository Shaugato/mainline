Real

  • Every SQL result on screen executed live against CockroachDB CCL v26.2.5, basic tier, in aws-ap-southeast-1.
  • The refusal is a database constraint — 23514 on gate_closed_when_issued — not application code. It refuses a cluster admin over raw SQL in the same way.
  • The MCP session is CockroachDB's public managed endpoint, read-only, and not our code. The EXPLAIN output is unedited.
  • Model inference runs in ap-southeast-2 (Sydney). Inference in Australia, database in Singapore. Sydney (ap-southeast-2) is Advanced-tier only and is absent from the Basic and Standard region lists, so end-to-end Australian data residency is false for this deployment and is not claimed anywhere.
  • G1 probe run of 2026-08-08T00:00:00Z: 2 capabilities probed, 2 measured as working, 0 measured as failing, absent or unmeasurable, and 1 questions recorded as unanswered rather than assumed.

Synthetic

  • Operator, sites, people, incidents and documents are generated: 4 sites, 140 people, 118 documents, 893 clauses across 2597 versions.
  • 1042 events and 441 blame edges; 106 events at severity 4 and 6 at severity 5. No real incident and no real person appears anywhere in this corpus.
  • Seed 0xMAINLINE, generator corpus-1.0.0. The corpus regenerates byte-identically from that seed.
  • Renderer census: 16 authored, 0 written by a model, 3607 templated — 0.0% of documents were model-written and 0 model-written words exist in the corpus. All 9214 words that appear on camera are hand-authored.
  • Embeddings are offline fixtures from minishlab/potion-base-8M, lifted to 1024 dimensions and stored as float16. They are not Titan, and this card says so rather than letting the demo imply otherwise.

Staged

  • Permit WO-88213 is pre-seeded to dispositioned with open_blocking = 1, so the beat fits three minutes.
  • Ingestion of the INC-2013-044 report ran earlier and is shown at 4x as an inset. It is sped up, never re-timed or re-ordered.
  • Commit dates are corpus data, not MVCC time travel. AS OF SYSTEM TIME cannot reach beyond the garbage-collection window, so it cannot reach 2013, and the long-horizon record is the application-level commit DAG. gc.ttlseconds is 4500 here — a 75-minute window.

The limits we cannot engineer away

Nothing here separates a considered disposition from a rubber stamp. We prove one was signed, by whom, against exactly which rows the same serializable transaction returned, and when. We measure deliberation and never threshold it. And we log every precursor we declined to surface, with its arithmetic.
