# READ THIS ALOUD — the voice pass, one block at a time

**This is the only page you need at the microphone.** Lines are printed clean, with no bold
markers to strip while you speak. Stress and pauses are beside the line, never inside it.

Record **in block order, one block per take**. If a block goes wrong, re-take that block only.

Total 2:52. **Never speed up to fit** — if a line will not fit at a comfortable pace, it is the
line that is wrong, and it gets cut before your delivery does.
